# Valuation Method Weights and Rating Framework

**Created by Jairo Alonso González**

## 1. No universal default weights

Method weights must be selected for the issuer and the quality/relevance of each method. The analyst must document the rationale, peer/method limitations and sensitivity to weights. Exclude inapplicable methods. Weights must be non-negative and sum to 100% if a blended valuation is presented.

Do not assign weight to technical indicators in a fundamental intrinsic-value blend. Technical analysis is a separate price-behavior context. Do not blend a technical price level with DCF or comparable valuation as if all measured the same economic quantity.

An illustrative mature operating-company blend might emphasize FCFF DCF and relevant trading comparables. A bank may instead emphasize P/B, P/E and residual-income/dividend methods. A conglomerate may require SOTP. These are selection examples, not preset weights.

## 2. Expected total return

For a defined horizon and consistent currency:

`Expected total return = (Base-case target price / Current price - 1) + Expected dividend per share over the horizon / Current price`

Do not add trailing dividend yield to a forward price-return estimate without explicitly explaining the mismatch. Consider buybacks separately only when the valuation method does not already incorporate their effect.

## 3. House rating thresholds

The following are an initial house framework, not universal market standards:

- **BUY / OVERWEIGHT:** expected total return ≥ +12%, and ROIC ≥ WACC where those measures are meaningful, with no unresolved material contradiction.
- **HOLD / NEUTRAL:** expected total return between −5% and +12%, or evidence is mixed but the thesis remains defensible.
- **SELL / UNDERWEIGHT:** expected total return < −5%, or material fundamental impairment is supported by evidence.
- **NOT RATED:** insufficient/contradictory evidence, unreliable price or share count, or missing critical valuation inputs. Do not force a rating.

Record rating horizon, current price timestamp, expected dividend assumption, ROIC/WACC basis and rationale.
